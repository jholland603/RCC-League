How this is calculated

Swingweight here is computed the classic way club builders do it by hand: it's a moment (torque) about a fixed 14-inch fulcrum from the butt end.

Raw points (weight × (balance − 14) − 6045.5) ÷ 49.61

Result is in points relative to D0. Rounding to the nearest whole point and mapping it onto the letter scale (…C9, D0, D1…) gives the grade.

Head suggestion

Assumes added mass sits at the club length you enter. A shorter lever arm than the true head CG will slightly understate the grams needed — treat it as a strong starting point, not a guarantee.

Butt suggestion

Assumes added mass sits 2″ from the butt (typical for tape under a grip), or your custom location if set.

What this doesn't capture

Swingweight is one number describing balance — it doesn't capture total moment of inertia or shaft loading. Two clubs can match on swingweight and still feel different if mass is distributed differently.
